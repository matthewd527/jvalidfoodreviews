I JUST Want TO EAT

Written, shot and eaten by Jean‑Philippe Gerbi

A Frenchman with a camera, a standing table reservation somewhere between Hoboken and Tribeca, and fifteen years of receipts. He started the blog in July 2011 for the simplest possible reason, and has not stopped since: every plate photographed by him, every room paid for and walked into unannounced.

No guest posts. No press releases. No place he has not actually eaten at.

07 — The Blogger

Fifteen years, one appetite

Jean-Philippe Gerbi is French, lives in the New York area, and started I Just Want To Eat in July 2011 because he liked eating and liked talking about eating rather more. Years of travel had already done the hard part: there is no cuisine he will not sit down to.

Nearly every photograph on the blog is his own, shot without flash so the colours survive, on a camera he carries everywhere. The logo was painted by his wife Jodi.

What he will not do is as much of the point as what he will. No sponsored guest posts. No advertorial. No review of a room he has not eaten in.
